# DDPR: Degradation-Decoupled Progressive Recovery for Lensless Imaging

Official repository for **"Degradation-Decoupled Progressive Recovery for Lensless Imaging."**

## Overview

DDPR is a two-stage framework for lensless image reconstruction:

- **Stage I:** Knowledge-guided model-mismatch compensation using cross-domain updates and spatially adaptive response modeling.
- **Stage II:** Stage-I-conditioned latent refinement for suppressing residual reconstruction artifacts and improving image quality.

The framework is evaluated on two public benchmarks (DiffuserCam and PhlatCam) and two self-built datasets covering challenging low-light and extreme near-field imaging conditions.

## Datasets

Our experiments involve two publicly available benchmarks and two self-built lensless imaging datasets.

**Public Benchmarks**

- **DiffuserCam:** Public lensless imaging benchmark.
- **PhlatCam:** Public phase-mask-based lensless imaging benchmark.

Please refer to the original dataset providers for access and usage conditions.

**Self-Built Datasets**

- **Low-Light Lensless Imaging Dataset:** Collected to evaluate reconstruction performance under low-light imaging conditions.
- **Extreme Near-Field Lensless Imaging Dataset:** Collected to investigate reconstruction performance under extreme near-field imaging conditions.

The two self-built datasets are maintained by our research team and are not available for unrestricted public download.
